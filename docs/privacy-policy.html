Privacy Policy

Hammer It! · Last updated: 6 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Hammer It! (package com.kinngshishupal.hammerit), developed by KinngShishupal.

Information we collect

Hammer It! does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising services, and does not send any data over the internet. Scores exist only while the app is open and are never uploaded.

Device permissions

Vibration: used only to give haptic feedback during gameplay. The app also declares the standard internet permission included with React Native apps, but the game makes no network requests.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party services that collect information about you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/KinngShishupal/HammerIt/issues.
